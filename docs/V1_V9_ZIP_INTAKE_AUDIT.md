## Art direction from v9 that Codex MUST enforce

- Overall `Cloudcrafted Fantasy` 2.5D (roughly 70% stylized/30% believable materials and light); warm key/cool cloud fill; cloudglass, cloudstone, brass/gold and enchanted wood.
- **Five gameplay tiles:** Cloud Puff, Mystic Bloom, Ember Drop, Dream Crystal and Golden Nugget. **No visible cannabis leaves except Secret Basement.** Keep Dart internal enum/save compatibility.
- MW crates and reinforced MW crates; **torch lighter** visually replaces Rocket; **Cloud Cup vaporizer** replaces hammer; Prism and Cloud Bomb remain approved visual specials; white Cloud Cup smoke.
- Mr. Weed identity and blank shirt; no crown or literal ruler motifs. Five Trolls remain **friendly** for V1.
- Economy UI labels: MW Coins, MW Bars and Cloud Energy. Dream Crystal is a tile, not currency. Do not quietly introduce economy mechanic changes.
- Collection frame ornate gold/wood, rarity color language (Common silver, Rare teal, Epic violet, Legendary gold), dynamic type/stats, player-state card/exhibit UI.
- All Lord Nave Von Elrekce, Copycat Cloud Co., betrayal/villain, rival Cloud Bottle, and explicit Keefler Elves revelations **later-game only**. Their concept art should not be packaged or shipped in the V1 Android assets.
