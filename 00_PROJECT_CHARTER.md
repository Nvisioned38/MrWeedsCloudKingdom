# 00 — Project Charter

## Product
**Mr. Weed's: Cloud Kingdom**  
Working tagline: **Match • Build • Collect • Explore**

## Vision
Create a cannabis-culture-themed casual game where Match-3 is the engine that powers a persistent, customizable world. The player should care about what happens outside the puzzle board as much as what happens on it.

## Competitive position
Cloud Kingdom combines:
- the world/story wrapper of renovation games,
- the collecting appeal of strain field guides,
- the long-term progression of idle/tycoon games,
- the accessibility and volume of classic Match-3,
- plus real customization, mini-games, secrets, visible collections, and player expression.

It must **not** become a storefront disguised as a game.

## Audience
Primary:
- Adults who already enjoy casual puzzle games.
- Fans of cannabis culture, humor, art, food, collectibles, and customization.
- Players who like short sessions but want persistent progression.

Secondary:
- Existing Mr. Weed's fans.
- Completionists and decorators.
- Players who prefer optional/offline play over competitive multiplayer.

## Product pillars
1. **Satisfying puzzle feel** — fast swaps, juicy cascades, readable goals.
2. **A world worth building** — upgrades are visible and player-directed.
3. **Collectibles with purpose** — every collectible has identity and utility.
4. **Variety beats grind** — mini-games, bosses, secrets, and event rules break repetition.
5. **Player-friendly economy** — no paywall level design and no forced-ad treadmill.
6. **Offline first** — core puzzle progression should work without a connection.
7. **Brand personality without becoming an ad** — Mr. Weed's supplies the universe, characters, humor, and aesthetics.

## Launch MVP
- 100 Match-3 levels
- 10 chapters
- 3 world areas
- 30 collectible strain cards
- 6 recurring characters
- 100+ decor item definitions
- 3 mini-game frameworks, with at least 1 fully playable at launch
- Daily challenge
- Collection museum
- Local offline save
- Optional cloud sync
- Optional rewarded ads only
- No PvP
- No chat
- No commerce
- No real cultivation instructions

## First playable vertical slice
The vertical slice is complete when a fresh install can:
1. Enter as a guest.
2. See the Cloud House exterior.
3. Play levels 1–10.
4. Complete move-based objectives.
5. Earn coins and stars.
6. Unlock one collectible strain card.
7. Spend stars to restore one Cloud House room.
8. Choose between at least 3 cosmetic room options.
9. Save everything locally.
10. Close/relaunch without losing progress.

## Launch quality bar
- No progression-loss bugs.
- No forced advertisements.
- No level requiring a purchase.
- 60 FPS target on current mainstream Android hardware.
- Deterministic board-generation support for reproducible tests.
- Accessibility: scalable text, color-independent tile distinctions, reduced-motion option, haptics toggle, sound/music controls.
